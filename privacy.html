Privacy

Wise Geospatial uses Google Analytics to understand general website activity, such as pages visited, referral sources and device information. This helps us improve the site and our services.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not sell personal information. If you contact us by email, phone, or our project-request form, the information you provide is used to respond to the inquiry and support the potential project.

Project-request forms use the third-party service FormSubmit to process and forward your name, email, company, selected project type, and message to Wise Geospatial. FormSubmit states that submission archives are retained for 30 days. Please do not include confidential, sensitive, or restricted project data in the first inquiry. Email us to arrange a suitable method for exchanging project files.

Some public field photographs may be resized and cached through an external image delivery service to reduce mobile download size.

You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings. For more information about Google’s data practices, visit Google’s Privacy Policy.
