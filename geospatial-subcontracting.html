COMMON QUESTIONS

Practical answers for production managers.

Can Wise process datasets collected by another firm?

Yes, when the supplied data, metadata, formats and quality requirements are sufficient. Scope and acceptance criteria are set before production starts.

Can we subcontract recurring remote production?

Yes. Individual work packages and recurring workload support can both be discussed, subject to current capacity and agreed scheduling.

Does Wise provide licensed survey or engineering certification?

No. Wise provides non-regulated field documentation and geospatial technical production. Boundary determinations, sealed plans, and other regulated services must be performed through appropriately licensed professionals and entities.

What are typical handoff formats?

Examples may include LAS/LAZ, DWG/DXF, georeferenced imagery, surfaces, GIS data, and PDF exhibits. Supported file formats, coordinate systems and standards are confirmed for each assignment.
