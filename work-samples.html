01 / CONSTRUCTION & MATERIALS

Earthwork quantities & progress comparisons

Repeatable capture and surface comparison packages designed to support contractor quantity reviews, stockpile monitoring and grading discussions.

Typical inputsTime-stamped imagery, control information, surface files, site extents and agreed reference surface.
Possible outputsOrthomosaic, surface models, cut/fill or stockpile summary, cross-sections, annotated review exhibit.
Scope & quality checks

Align measurement limits, units, coordinate basis, source dates, surface boundaries, voids and exclusions before calculation. Quantities are for agreed project support uses, not a substitute for independent professional certification.

02 / LIDAR & REALITY CAPTURE

Point-cloud production & terrain extraction

A structured handoff for survey, engineering and mapping teams that already have scan or LiDAR data and need production capacity.

Typical inputsLAS/LAZ or scan files, control and metadata, classification rules, coordinate system and project standards.
Possible outputsOrganized point cloud, classification support, ground surface, contours, data QA and file delivery manifest.
Scope & quality checks

Check units, coordinate reference, extents, point density, classification exceptions and completeness. Registration and accuracy requirements must be agreed with the licensed or responsible project team where applicable.

03 / CORRIDORS & INFRASTRUCTURE

ROW mapping & GIS production support

Convert existing imagery, alignment and asset records into organized exhibits and GIS data for project coordination.

Typical inputsAlignment or route data, GIS features, imagery, existing record documents and deliverable schema.
Possible outputsCorridor mapping exhibits, GIS layers, CAD linework, indexed features and organized supporting files.
Scope & quality checks

Document source provenance, coordinate systems, feature attributes, discrepancies and items requiring field or professional verification. Mapping support does not establish property boundaries or legal rights.
