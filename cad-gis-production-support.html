Common questions

Can you work remotely?

Yes. Production support is available nationwide using client-provided data. Field capture is scoped separately for San Antonio and Central/South Texas.

Do you provide licensed surveying or engineering?

Wise provides technical production support. Boundary determinations, certified surveys and engineering design require the appropriate licensed professional and review structure.

What determines the price and turnaround?

Input quality, file size, deliverables, project standards and revision requirements. A representative sample lets us scope the work before quoting.

Can you help with related data?

Explore LiDAR and point-cloud processing, construction quantity tracking or UAS site documentation.
