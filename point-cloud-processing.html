COMMON QUESTIONS

Point-cloud processing FAQ.

What files should be included with a pilot work package?

Start with a representative LAS/LAZ, imagery set, CAD/GIS file or sample deliverable, plus the coordinate reference, units, target output and any QA checklist.

Can Wise work inside an established production workflow?

Yes. Defined stages such as cleanup, classification support, derivative preparation, GIS/CAD conversion and delivery QA can be scoped around your existing naming and review conventions.

Can processed data be used for certified survey or engineering decisions?

Technical production support is not a substitute for licensed survey, engineering or other regulated professional judgment. Certified or regulated deliverables require the appropriate licensed structure and review process.

How do we begin without handing over a large backlog?

Use a small pilot: one representative dataset, a target output and a deadline. It gives both teams a chance to confirm inputs, outputs and QA expectations before expanding scope.

CLEAR SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Production support that respects the responsible professional.

Wise Geospatial provides technical spatial-data processing and production support. Deliverables are prepared from available input data and project direction; they are not a substitute for licensed survey, engineering, geologic, legal or other regulated professional judgment.

Where a project requires certified accuracy, boundary determination, engineering design or regulated survey deliverables, work is performed only within the appropriate licensed structure and under the project’s required review process.
