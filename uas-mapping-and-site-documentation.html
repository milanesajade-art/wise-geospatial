COMMON QUESTIONS

UAS mapping and site-documentation FAQ.

What is the difference between drone documentation and a land survey?

Drone documentation records visible conditions and imagery. It is not a boundary survey, certified map or regulated accuracy deliverable unless the work is performed within the required licensed survey structure.

What files can a project team receive?

A scope can include organized high-resolution images, repeatable progress views, web-ready media, orthomosaic or 3D-model support, and files packaged for GIS, CAD or technical review.

How quickly can site documentation be delivered?

Many standard capture packages are delivered within a typical 48–72-hour window after a successful flight, depending on site access, airspace, weather and processing scope.

What is needed before a mapping-support flight?

Send the location, target date, intended use, site access/contact, priority features, required deliverable and any available control or accuracy requirement.

CLEAR SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Useful visual and spatial support—without overstating what it is.

Wise Geospatial provides UAS capture, imagery, documentation and spatial-data production support. Deliverables document visible conditions and supplied project information; they do not provide a land survey, engineering design, roof certification, inspection, appraisal or legal boundary opinion.

Where licensed survey control, regulated accuracy, boundary determination or professional certification is required, work is performed only within the appropriate licensed structure.
