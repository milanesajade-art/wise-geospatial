CLEAR LIMITATIONS

Documentation is evidence—not a professional opinion.

Aerial and property-condition imagery can make visible conditions easier to review and communicate. It does not replace a licensed home inspection, engineering evaluation, roof certification, appraisal, legal boundary survey or title opinion.

Drone operations also depend on site conditions, airspace, weather, access and a safe legal flight plan. A good scope identifies those constraints before capture day.

COMMON QUESTIONS

San Antonio drone documentation FAQ.

How quickly can files be delivered?

Many standard property packages can be delivered within about 48 hours after a successful capture, depending on scope and editing.

Can you photograph a roof?

Yes, when the site, airspace, weather and flight plan allow it. Images document visible conditions; they are not a roof certification or engineering opinion.

Do you cover commercial property?

Yes. Common subjects include frontage, parking, circulation, roof areas, loading zones, facades and overall site context.

What about ranches and acreage?

Larger tracts usually need a planned sequence of launch locations and priority features instead of a standard residential image count.

Can parcel lines be added?

Supplied parcel or boundary information can be shown as approximate visual context when clearly labeled. It is not a boundary determination or survey.

What should I send for a quote?

Property location, acreage, intended use, target date, priority features, occupancy or access notes and desired photo, video or documentation outputs.
