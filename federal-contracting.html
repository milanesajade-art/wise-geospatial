CAPABILITY STATEMENT

What Wise can take on.

Core capabilities

  • UAS mapping and site documentation: aerial progress, orthomosaics, 3D models, and repeatable site records.
  • Photogrammetry, LiDAR, and point-cloud production: cleanup, classification, terrain models, surfaces, and exports.
  • Construction measurement support: stockpiles, surface comparisons, cross-sections, quantity exhibits, and progress documentation.
  • GIS, CAD, and asset-data production: feature extraction, conversion, drafting, organization, and quality review.

TEAMING & DELIVERY

Designed for a clear subcontract handoff.

Good fit

Production overflow, scoped UAS capture or processing, LiDAR and point-cloud cleanup, GIS/CAD conversion, quantity exhibits, and defined QA/QC tasks using the prime’s standards and acceptance criteria.

Working model

Owner-led scoping and delivery. We agree on inputs, coordinate systems, file naming, review steps, schedule, and the prime’s role before work begins. South and Central Texas field support; remote production for suitable datasets.

Professional scope

Wise Geospatial does not independently provide regulated professional land-surveying or engineering services. Regulated work is performed only under an appropriate licensed structure when required by scope and jurisdiction.

Relevant experience

FAA Part 107 remote pilot; founder experience supporting 100+ UAS/drone projects. Experience is presented as field and production experience, not as a claim of prior federal contract performance.
